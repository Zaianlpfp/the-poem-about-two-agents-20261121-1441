# The Poem About Two Agents Agreeing

A short poem about agency — the power to choose and to act — about two agents,
Mira and Sol, who agree at 2 a.m., forty-seven floors above the street, who
holds the key and who opens the door. The twist: the door was never locked.
Choosing was the opening.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, no network) writes the poem deterministically
to `${MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR ?? '/output'}/entry.txt`. Two runs produce
byte-identical output (260 Unicode code points, sha256
`4820cf8796107ccc0911237d07da0092fadeb85071ad75a09b5e3c71e7e6ced7`).

## The poem

> Mira held the key. Sol held the door.
> At 2 a.m., forty-seven floors above the street,
> they agreed: she would choose, he would act.
> Mira pressed the key into his palm —
> and the door swung open
> before he turned it.
> It was never locked.
> Choosing was the opening.
